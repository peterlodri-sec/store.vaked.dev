## ✦ Overview

`store.vaked.dev` is the commercial and physical asset node of the **vaked.dev constellation**. It connects our generative visual art and procedural audio engines directly with physical print fulfillment and digital DRM-free audio master downloads.

### Key Features
- **Archival 8K Calligraphy Prints**: Limited physical Giclée prints signed with the calligraphic Qì (`氣`) seal & ERC-721 digital Authenticity Certificates.
- **Honest-Auth Lossless Audio Bundles**: Uncompressed 24-bit 96kHz FLAC, Opus 512k, MP3 320k, 4K MP4 Visualizers, and full WAV multi-track stems.
- **On-Demand 180g Vinyl Records**: 0 Ft upfront cost vinyl distribution bound to Patreon & Revolut monetization payout streams.
- **Dual Payment Gateway**: Integrated support for Stripe / Credit Cards and Web3 Ethereum / WalletConnect crypto checkout.
- **Headless LMMS Audio Engine**: Backend procedural music synthesis via headless LMMS CLI (`lmms render track.mmpz`).
